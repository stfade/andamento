A PALETTE · VERSION 0.1

Andamento

A palette for tools that prove their work. Verdigris on travertine, one colour per meaning, and gold only when two halves agree.

◧ one half → ■ both halves · gold appears nowhere else

FOUR RULES OF THE FLOOR

01

One tile is not the picture

A colour carries one meaning. It is never there to fill space.

02

Cut to fit

The palette stays small. Add a colour only when a meaning has no home.

03

The grout holds

Hairlines carry the structure. Boxes are the exception, not the default.

04

Gold is rare

Gold marks agreement between two halves. Anywhere else it stops meaning anything.

TYPE & MATERIAL

One family. A monospace, nothing else.
Labels. 9.5px, uppercase, letter-spacing .18em.
Body. 13px, line-height 19px, tabular numerals.
Motion. 120ms; only when a state settles. No spinners.
Surfaces. Hairlines, not boxes. Grout is structure.
Density. 100×30 first; no scrolling in the main view.

THE MATCH

+
→

ONE HALF + THE OTHER → GOLD.
A half proves nothing on its own. Gold is the moment two halves agree - and the only moment the palette allows it.

WHAT THIS FORBIDS

✗A colour without a meaning.
✗A badge or progress bar that exists to look busy.
✗A mosaic pattern used as wallpaper.
✗Gold before the match.
